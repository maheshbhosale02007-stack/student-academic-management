# Agile Product Vision Document

## Vision

Develop a secure and user-friendly Student Record and Academic Management Platform that enables educational institutions to efficiently manage student records, attendance, academic performance, course registration, and reporting through a scalable web-based application.

## Product Objective

Translate the Unit-1 vision into an incremental Agile product that delivers valuable functionality in small, testable releases.

## Primary Users

- Students
- Faculty
- Academic Administrators
- Examination Cell Staff
- IT Support

## Business Benefits

- Centralized academic information
- Reduced manual work
- Better data accuracy
- Faster reporting
- Improved visibility for students
- Controlled access to sensitive records
- Scalable foundation for future integrations

## Success Criteria

1. Core user workflows are available through the MVP.
2. High-priority backlog items meet their acceptance criteria.
3. Users can complete common tasks with minimal navigation.
4. Academic data is protected through role-based access.
5. Reports can be generated from centralized records.

## Project Constraints

- Time-boxed academic project
- Limited initial scope
- Data privacy requirements
- Technical dependencies among modules
- Need for iterative stakeholder validation

## Expected Capabilities

- Authentication and role management
- Student registration and profile management
- Course management and enrollment
- Attendance recording and reporting
- Marks and grade management
- Notifications
- Academic reporting
