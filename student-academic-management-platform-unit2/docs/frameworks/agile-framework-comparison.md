# Agile Framework Comparison Report

## Comparison

| Framework | Strengths | Limitations | Suitability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scrum | Clear roles, sprints, reviews, retrospectives | Requires disciplined ceremonies | **High** |
| Kanban | Flexible flow, visual work management | Less prescriptive planning structure | Medium |
| XP | Strong engineering practices and frequent feedback | Requires mature technical discipline | High for engineering quality |
| Lean Development | Waste reduction and value focus | Less prescriptive team structure | Medium |
| LeSS | Scales Scrum across multiple teams | More coordination complexity | Low initially |
| SAFe | Provides large-scale enterprise coordination | Heavy framework for a small academic team | Low initially |
| Scrum@Scale | Scales Scrum through interconnected teams | More organizational overhead | Low initially |

## Recommended Framework — Scrum

Scrum is the most suitable initial framework because the project can be developed by a small cross-functional team in incremental sprints. The product has clearly identifiable modules and stakeholder feedback can be incorporated during Sprint Reviews.

## Why Not Start with a Scaling Framework?

LeSS, SAFe, and Scrum@Scale are designed for larger or multi-team environments. A small academic project does not require their additional coordination structures.

## Supporting Practice

The team can adopt selected XP engineering practices such as automated testing, continuous integration, pair review, and refactoring without changing the overall Scrum framework.
