# Scrum Roles and Responsibilities

## Product Owner

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate product requirements.
- Prioritize the Product Backlog.
- Represent stakeholder interests.
- Clarify acceptance expectations.
- Accept completed work.

### Success Measure
The backlog maximizes product value and clearly communicates what should be built next.

## Scrum Master

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate Scrum ceremonies.
- Help remove impediments.
- Support effective collaboration.
- Encourage adherence to Scrum practices.
- Help the team improve continuously.

### Success Measure
The team can work effectively and continuously improve its process.

## Development Team

### Responsibilities
- Design application components.
- Implement user stories.
- Write and execute tests.
- Prepare technical documentation.
- Demonstrate completed features.

### Success Measure
The team produces a usable, tested increment that satisfies the Definition of Done.

## Stakeholder Interaction

The Product Owner represents stakeholder needs, while Sprint Reviews provide opportunities for stakeholders to inspect increments and provide feedback.
