# Scrum Team Structure

## Team Composition

| Role | Suggested Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Product Owner | Owns product value and backlog priority |
| Scrum Master | Facilitates Scrum and removes impediments |
| Development Team | Designs, develops, tests, and documents the product |

## Collaboration Model

```text
                    Product Owner
                         |
                         v
                Product Backlog
                         |
                         v
                   Scrum Team
                /              \
       Scrum Master       Development Team
             |                    |
             +---------+----------+
                       |
                       v
                 Sprint Increment
                       |
                       v
                Sprint Review
                       |
                       v
                  Feedback
                       |
                       +----> Backlog refinement
```

## Team Working Agreement

- Communicate blockers early.
- Keep the backlog updated.
- Review work before considering it Done.
- Respect role responsibilities.
- Focus on the sprint goal.
- Use evidence and stakeholder feedback for product decisions.
