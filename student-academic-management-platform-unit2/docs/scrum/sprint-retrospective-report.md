# Sprint Retrospective Report

## Sprint

Sprint 1

## What Went Well

- Repository structure was established early.
- Roles and responsibilities were clear.
- User stories provided a common understanding of expected behavior.
- Acceptance criteria helped define Done.

## What Could Be Improved

- Some estimates may have uncertainty.
- Technical dependencies should be identified earlier.
- Testing should begin during implementation rather than only at the end.
- Daily Scrum should focus on blockers and sprint progress.

| Improve backlog refinement | Product Owner + Team | Ongoing |

## Retrospective Principle

The purpose of the retrospective is continuous improvement of both the product and the team's way of working.
