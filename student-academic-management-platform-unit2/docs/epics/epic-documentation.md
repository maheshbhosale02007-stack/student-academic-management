# Epic Documentation

## Epic 1 — Student Management

**Purpose:** Maintain accurate student information.

**Features:**
- Student Registration
- Student Profile Management
- Student Search
- Student Update
- Student Deletion

**Value:** Creates a centralized source of student records.

## Epic 2 — Course Management

**Purpose:** Manage courses and student-course relationships.

**Features:**
- Add Course
- Update Course
- Assign Students
- View Course Details

**Value:** Provides structured course information and enrollment.

## Epic 3 — Attendance Management

**Purpose:** Digitize attendance tracking.

**Features:**
- Record Attendance
- View Attendance
- Attendance Reports

**Value:** Reduces manual attendance processing and improves visibility.

## Epic 4 — Marks and Academic Performance

**Purpose:** Manage marks and academic performance.

**Features:**
- Enter Marks
- Update Marks
- Calculate Grades
- Generate Result Sheets

**Value:** Improves consistency and efficiency of academic performance management.

## Epic 5 — Authentication and Security

**Purpose:** Protect academic information.

**Features:**
- User Login
- Logout
- Password Reset
- Role-Based Access Control

**Value:** Restricts access to authorized users and roles.

## Epic 6 — Notifications

**Purpose:** Keep users informed of important academic events.

**Features:**
- Attendance Alerts
- Examination Notifications
- Result Notifications

**Value:** Improves timely communication.

## Epic 7 — Reporting

**Purpose:** Provide useful academic reports.

**Features:**
- Student Reports
- Attendance Reports
- Performance Reports
- Course Reports

**Value:** Reduces manual reporting effort and improves decision-making.
