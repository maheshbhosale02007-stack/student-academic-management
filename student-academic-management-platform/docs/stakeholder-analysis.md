# Stakeholder Analysis Report

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Main Needs | Engagement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Students | High | Medium | Records, attendance, results, reports | Frequent feedback |
| Faculty | High | High | Class lists, attendance, academic entry | Workshops + pilot |
| Academic Administrators | High | High | Record management, reports, approvals | Product decisions |
| Examination Cell | High | High | Results, verification, reports | Workflow validation |
| IT Support | Medium | High | Security, availability, maintenance | Technical reviews |
| Institution Management | Medium | High | Efficiency, compliance, outcomes | Periodic reviews |

## Stakeholder Priorities

1. Protect student data.
2. Improve record accuracy.
3. Reduce manual administrative work.
4. Make common tasks easy to complete.
5. Provide reliable reporting.

## Communication Plan

- Students: surveys and usability testing.
- Faculty: interviews and workflow workshops.
- Administrators: requirement workshops.
- Examination cell: process mapping and validation.
- IT support: architecture and security reviews.
