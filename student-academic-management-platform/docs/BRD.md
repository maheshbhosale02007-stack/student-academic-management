# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Business Context

Higher-education institutions manage large volumes of student information across admissions, registration, attendance, examinations, results, and academic reporting. Disconnected or manual processes make it difficult to maintain consistent and timely records.

## 2. Business Problem

The current process may involve spreadsheets, paper documents, emails, and multiple disconnected records. Key problems include:

- Duplicate or inconsistent student information
- Manual attendance calculations
- Delayed result processing
- Difficulty locating historical academic records
- Time-consuming report preparation
- Limited transparency for students
- Higher risk of unauthorized access

## 3. Business Goal

Create a centralized academic management platform that improves accuracy, accessibility, security, and efficiency of student record management.

## 4. Objectives

- Centralize student academic records.
- Reduce manual data entry and duplication.
- Allow faculty to record attendance efficiently.
- Support examination and result workflows.
- Generate academic reports quickly.
- Provide students with controlled access to their records.
- Maintain secure, role-based access.

## 5. Stakeholders

Students, faculty members, academic administrators, examination cell staff, and IT support personnel.

## 6. Business Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| BR-01 | Maintain a centralized student record | High |
| BR-02 | Support student registration | High |
| BR-03 | Manage courses and enrollment | High |
| BR-04 | Record and review attendance | High |
| BR-05 | Manage examination results | High |
| BR-06 | Generate academic reports | High |
| BR-07 | Enforce role-based security | High |
| BR-08 | Maintain auditability of important changes | Medium |
| BR-09 | Support future scalability | Medium |

## 7. Success Criteria

- Student records can be created and retrieved without duplicate manual records.
- Faculty can submit attendance through a standardized workflow.
- Authorized staff can generate academic reports.
- Students can view their own academic information.
- Unauthorized users cannot access restricted records.
- The platform remains usable as the number of students and courses grows.

## 8. Assumptions

- Users have institutional accounts.
- The institution provides course, student, and examination data.
- Users have access to a web browser and institutional network/internet.
- Policies for academic data retention and access are defined by the institution.

## 9. Constraints

- Academic data is sensitive and requires controlled access.
- The first release has limited scope.
- Integration with existing institutional systems may be required later.

## 10. Out of Scope for MVP

- Fee/payment management
- Hostel management
- Library management
- Transport management
- Parent portal
- Advanced predictive analytics
- Native mobile applications
